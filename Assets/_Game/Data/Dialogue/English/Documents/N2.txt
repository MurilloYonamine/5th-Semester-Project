Title: Torn Notebook
---
Francisco asked me to destroy the source
as his last request, but something went wrong...
Everyone is acting strange. The whole village.
Smile when they smile. Don't take the medicine.
I found three other people
who are also seeing something wrong here.
Leonardo, Clara, and Denise.
We don't talk much, we only write notes.
It seems hard for those things to read
when they assimilate with humans.
If you're like us, we need to reach the source.

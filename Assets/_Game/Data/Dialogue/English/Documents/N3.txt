Author: Dr. Leonardo
Estimated date: May 2, 19XX
We came to Mycoris Valley to investigate a herbal preparation used for generations.
The villagers swear it cures fevers, aches, and infections within hours.
We were welcomed warmly, almost too warmly...
Also, since yesterday, a dense fog has covered all the roads.
We will remain at the hospital until the weather improves.
Communication is unstable. Leaving is impossible.

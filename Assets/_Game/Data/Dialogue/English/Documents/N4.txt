Title: Torn Diary
---
...isco Benedito
February 23, 198X
The land is fertile beyond normal, and something
in that forest catches my attention.
The spring water speeds recovery like magic.
The animals come frequently,
as if they knew the mutual benefit of going there.
Not to ambush, but to seek peace.
This place is enough to make me conclude it will be
a good place to establish a hospital in the region.
If this spring can cure him,
then this place will change everything...

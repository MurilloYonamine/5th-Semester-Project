...If anyone is reading this,
I will no longer be Francisco.
Something in that spring took my voice,
my limbs, my senses...
To the point that it feels like I'm
wearing another man's body.
Now I realize I didn't cure Helena,
I cursed her for the rest of her life.
In a world without family,
emotions, or feelings.
Do not trust anyone
who took the medicine.
When they started smiling,
that spring changed.
And it's stronger at nightfall.
Close the hospital
and do what I didn't:
BURN THAT DAMNED SPRING.
Forgive me...

Author: Dr. Clara
Estimated date: May 6, 19XX
Today they celebrated the discovery of the spring beside the hospital,
treated by the villagers as sacred.
They say the first flowers used in the local medicine came from there.
We cataloged the species provisionally as Nebulis sp. PHOTOSSYNC.
When closed, they look like ordinary flowers.
When open, they resemble human hands rising from the ground.
I observed a strong contraction of the stem under direct light at nightfall.
I recommended suspending the tests. I'm waiting for answers.

Title: Torn Diary
---
Author: Francisco Benedito
May 7, 199X
A group of researchers came to the region
to study our medicinal plant.
Their leader, Leonardo, insisted.
At first I thought it was bad, but to my delight,
they showed me new perspectives.
I was impressed by our miraculous flower.
They gave it an elegant name:
PHOTOSSYNC.
Interesting...
though I prefer what the younger ones called it:
LightSeek.
Whatever the name,
I believe it will bring prosperity to this village.

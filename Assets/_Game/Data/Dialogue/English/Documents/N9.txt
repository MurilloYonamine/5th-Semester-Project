Title: Report Document
---
Author: Dr. Clara
Estimated date: May 12, 199X
Doctors Bento and Elize
were found dead this morning, smiling.
An unusually severe progression for a common flu.
I believe I contracted the same disease as them.
Subtle hallucinations, voices in my head,
and a lack of sensation.
There is something wrong in this place,
something far worse than a common outbreak.
